The Threshold is a psychological horror game. Trapped in an endless time loop, your only goal is to escape. However, with each new attempt, the environment fractures. You will need to survive anomalies that make each room unique, escalating from subtle oddities to violent manifestations.

You take on the role of Paul, a father shattered after the tragic murder of his daughter at the hands of his own wife. Now, cornered in this temporal purgatory, he is forced to relive the echoes of his trauma over and over again. To find the way out, you will have to confront the environment, unearth dark truths repressed in the past, and discover if you are, in fact, alone on this journey.
